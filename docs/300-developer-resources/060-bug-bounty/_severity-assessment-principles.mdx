1. Severity is set by the worst impact the Report credibly demonstrates, not the theoretical worst case of the affected code path. Claimed impacts that are not demonstrated will be rated conservatively.
2. Impacts requiring unrealistic preconditions (including control of on-chain governance, a supermajority of node operators, physical access, or victim cooperation beyond normal platform usage) may be downgraded or deemed ineligible. This includes cases where the attacker's key must already be added to the target identity or account with some level of permission, such as an existing secondary key or agent escalating beyond its granted access: these are rated Medium or Low unless the finding bypasses the underlying authorization or approval prerequisite completely.
3. For Confidential Assets findings, two additional impact classes apply: a **soundness break** (creating, inflating, or overdrawing confidential balances) is classified as Critical; a **confidentiality break** (recovering transaction amounts, asset identities, or counterparties without the corresponding decryption keys) is classified as Critical or High, depending on its generality and prerequisites.
4. Vulnerabilities in the Revive/EVM contracts layer, or in shared security mechanisms that accept both Substrate and Ethereum-style keys (MultiSig, the Relayer/Subsidizer), are assessed by analogy to the classes above and subject to the prerequisites principle in item 2, and in particular include: a **privileged-action bypass** (an unauthorized caller, holding no relevant key or permission on the identity or account, executing an agent- or owner-gated action such as minting, freezing, forced transfer, or pausing without the authorization the equivalent native operation requires), classified as Critical or High depending on the privilege bypassed, and capped at Medium or Low where the caller's key is already added to the identity and the finding is really a permission escalation rather than a complete bypass; an **account or identity confusion** (a call or key resolving to the wrong asset, account, or identity across the EVM/Substrate boundary or between key types), classified as Critical if it enables unauthorized asset movement or impersonation of an identity the caller has no rights to; and an **unauthorized fee or subsidy charge** (fees or subsidy allowance drawn from a party that did not authorize it, or drawn beyond the limit that party set), classified as Medium or High depending on scale. A subsidized key spending its own authorized allowance through ordinary transaction activity is expected behavior, not a finding.
5. A Participant may include a CVSS vector or other scoring methodology in a Report as supporting context, but no score or methodology creates any entitlement to a particular classification or Bounty Payout.
